# Business Insights and Recommendations

**Dataset:** Microsoft AdventureWorks Sales (fictional sample data)  
**Period:** July 1, 2017 – June 15, 2020  
**Measure definition:** Gross Profit = Sales Amount − Total Product Cost

## 1. Revenue concentration in Bikes

**Finding:** Bikes contribute about $94.62M, approximately 86.2% of overall revenue.

**Implication:** Performance is sensitive to the Bikes category.

**Suggested investigation:** Review growth and risk across other categories; evaluate opportunities to diversify sales without assuming lower-revenue categories can scale at current margins.

## 2. Channel profitability disparity

**Finding:** Internet gross margin is around 41.15%, while Reseller gross margin is around 0.58%.

**Implication:** Revenue alone gives an incomplete picture of channel performance.

**Suggested investigation:** Compare pricing, product assortment, negotiated reseller terms, and recorded product costs by channel. Do not infer net profit from gross margin.

## 3. Reseller losses in Touring and Road Bikes

**Finding:** Together, reseller Touring and Road Bikes generate approximately $2.41M in gross losses. Certain Touring-1000 variants contribute substantial losses.

**Implication:** These products are candidates for targeted pricing/cost review.

**Suggested investigation:** Examine margins at model and variant level, price history, reseller agreements, cost allocation, and volume before implementing changes.

## 4. Geographic profitability and channel mix

**Finding:** Australia has a 33.57% gross margin and receives approximately 85.04% of sales revenue from the Internet channel. Countries with lower Internet shares, such as the United States and Canada, have substantially lower overall gross margins.

**Implication:** Channel mix is associated with country-level profitability; product mix and pricing may also matter.

**Suggested investigation:** Compare channel-adjusted regional margins and category composition before attributing differences to any one factor.

## 5. High-margin Accessories

**Finding:** Accessories have the highest category gross margin, approximately 49.88%, but low relative revenue.

**Implication:** There may be an opportunity to improve the revenue mix, subject to demand and operational constraints.

**Suggested investigation:** Test accessory cross-selling with Bike purchases, review customer demand, and estimate incremental profit rather than focusing only on percentage margin.
